# Privacy Policy — Fit Cricket

_Last updated: August 24, 2026_

Fit Cricket ("the app", "we", "us") helps you record cricket matches, track your
scoring and estimated calories burned, and sync that data across your devices.
This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to run your account and the app's
features.

**Account and profile information (you provide):**
- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Preferred playing role (batter, bowler, all-rounder, wicket-keeper)
- An optional "About Me" description
- An optional profile photo

**Cricket activity you create in the app:**
- Teams and player rosters you set up
- Match records, scorecards, and results
- Estimated calories burned per match (derived from your batting, bowling, and
  fielding activity — an approximation for fun, not a medical measurement)

**Technical information:**
- An authentication session token, stored securely on your device to keep you
  signed in.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, health-app data, advertising
identifiers, or usage-tracking analytics.

## How we use your information

- To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
- To store your cricket data and sync it across the devices where you sign in
  with the same account.
- To show matches you took part in — including matches recorded by another
  participant — on your home feed and live-scoreboard views.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for
advertising.

## Information visible to other players

Cricket is a team activity, so some data is shared with the other participants
of a match:

- When you (or another player) record a match, the roster stores each player's
  **name and phone number** so the match can appear on every participant's home
  feed.
- Any signed-in user whose phone number is listed in a match roster can view
  that match's scoreboard, including the other players' names on that roster.

Only include a player's phone number if you have their permission to do so.

## How your data is stored and protected

- Your data is stored locally on your device and synced to our backend provider,
  **Supabase** (which hosts the database and authentication on our behalf).
- Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
- Access is restricted by server-side security rules so that, aside from the
  shared match data described above, you can only read and write your own
  records.
- Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored by
  the app in plain text.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion
of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at
**[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We will delete your profile and the records you own; match
data that other participants also rely on may be retained in anonymized or
reduced form where required to keep their records intact.

## Children's privacy

Fit Cricket is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in
your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will
delete it.
