Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
Yes — a free account lets your teams, matches, and stats sync across your devices.
How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password" to receive a reset link by email.
Does it work offline?
Yes. You can score matches with no connection; your data syncs automatically once you're back online.
How are calories calculated?
Calorie figures are estimates based on your in-game batting, bowling, and fielding activity. They're meant for fun and general motivation, not medical or fitness measurement.
How do I delete my account?
Email us at the address above and we'll delete your account and associated personal data.
